# Stick Labs

Free stick tuning for your controller: curves, deadzones, anti-deadzone, button remaps, polling rate and more, with your sticks moving live on screen.

**Supported:** GameSir G7 Pro 8K · GameSir Tarantula 8K · LeadJoy Xeno Pro · LeadJoy Saber Plus · HyperStrike · Flydigi Apex 5

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest version for Windows](https://github.com/Realkiyoshii/stick-labs/releases/latest)**

Pick `Stick-Labs-setup-x.y.z.exe` (installs and updates itself) or `Stick-Labs-portable-x.y.z.exe` (no install).

## Use it in your browser

**https://realkiyoshii.github.io/stick-labs/**: same app, no install (Chrome or Edge).

## Stick Labs Pro

**[Get Pro: $6.99 one-time, no subscription](https://kiyoshii8.gumroad.com/l/sticklabspro)**

An optional unlock for the Windows desktop app. Everything above stays free.

- **Unlimited profiles:** save as many setups as you want on your PC and load any of them into a controller slot.
- **Game auto-switch:** open a game and your controller switches to its slot or loads its profile. Works with any game.
- **Playtime:** see how long you play each game, and with which profile.
- **Stream overlay:** live sticks with trails and triggers on your stream, as an OBS or Streamlabs Browser Source.
- **Your own theme:** turn any GIF or image into a full Stick Labs theme.

You get a license key by email: paste it into Stick Labs → Pro. One key works on up to 2 PCs. Profile library and auto-switch support GameSir G7-family controllers and the HyperStrike.
